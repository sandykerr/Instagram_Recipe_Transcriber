If you love white rice, one serving of this is almost 40 grams of protein and 30 grams of fiber.
To pair with your rice, start with a selection of beans and lentils. These will give you a range
of different soluble and insoluble fibers as well as resistant starches to allow deep
fermentation in your colon and a handy dose of protein to go with that fiber. Throw these into
your pan and give those naughty dirty beans a good wash and this can help to slightly lower their
FODMAP content, good for those sensitive guts. I'm using a pressure cooker because I'm
extra but you can use a rice cooker too. Now you could stop here and you've got a perfectly easy
to make high fiber high protein dish but you can go another level to really throw a party for those
microbes inside you. Throw in some frozen spinach, garlic, ginger loaded with polyphenols and bioactive
compounds that support microbial diversity and help calm inflammation. Onions and chili so I can finally
feel something warm in my cold heart. A grandma's dosing of ghee to help mop up those fat soluble
vitamins. A lot of people try to make a battle between protein and fiber but these two macronutrients
each have their own role and there are plenty of ways to include both in your diet. And no,
plant protein is not inferior to animal protein. This is my version of the beautiful Indian dish
Kichdi and it has all the essential amino acids you need and plenty of fiber so you can poop like a king.
